A Simple Content Plan for a New Blog

Build a sustainable publishing routine with topics readers need.

A consistent blog starts with a manageable plan.

Choose your audience

Define who you want to help and what questions they have.

Pick three content pillars

For a technology blog, these could be AI tools, beginner tech guides and digital skills.

Plan four articles

Start with one helpful article per week. Prioritize quality over volume.

Use a checklist

Before publishing, check accuracy, grammar, headings, links and mobile readability.

Measure what matters

Review which topics help readers and revise your plan as you learn.
