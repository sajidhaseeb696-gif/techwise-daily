How to Spot Online Earning Scams

Warning signs to check before spending time or money.

Some online job offers are designed to collect money or personal information rather than provide work.

Be cautious of upfront payments

A request to pay to unlock wages or withdraw earnings is a serious warning sign.

Check the company

Look for independent information, clear terms and a real support channel.

Question unrealistic claims

Promises of high guaranteed returns for almost no work are not credible.

Protect personal data

Never share one-time passwords or account login details with strangers.

Verify withdrawal rules

Read payout methods, minimum balances and geographic eligibility before starting.
