5 Practical Ways to Use AI in Your Daily Work

Simple ideas to save time while keeping your work accurate and original.

AI tools can help with everyday work, but the best results come from clear instructions and careful review.

1. Brainstorm ideas

Ask for several approaches to a topic, then choose and refine the ideas that fit your goals.

2. Improve first drafts

Use AI to reorganize rough notes into an outline. Check facts and rewrite in your own voice.

3. Summarize your notes

Turn meeting notes or study material into a concise summary, and compare it with the original.

4. Plan your week

Create a realistic checklist and prioritize important tasks.

5. Practice new skills

Ask for exercises, examples and feedback. Never share private passwords or sensitive customer data.

Final thought

AI is a useful assistant, not a replacement for judgment. Verify important information before acting on it.
