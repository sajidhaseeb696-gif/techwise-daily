SEO Basics: Make Your Blog Easier to Find

Understand search intent, titles, headings and useful content.

Search engine optimization helps people discover useful pages. It is a long-term process, not an instant traffic trick.

Understand search intent

Write for the question a reader is trying to answer. Include clear examples and useful next steps.

Write descriptive titles

Choose a title that accurately represents the article. Avoid exaggerated promises.

Use clear headings

Organize your article with logical sections so readers can scan it easily.

Improve technical basics

Use mobile-friendly layouts, fast-loading pages, descriptive links and meaningful image alt text.

Review and update

Keep your guides accurate as products and platforms change.
